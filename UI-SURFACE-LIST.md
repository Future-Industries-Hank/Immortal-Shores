**R3 pass notes (2026-08-03) — judge R2 findings (UI 6.4, map 5.3, mobile 4.1):**

- **Mobile blowout root cause found & fixed:** `#hud-top` / `#hub` are grid items of `#game` with default `min-width:auto`; `#nav`'s nowrap min-content (~460px) widened the whole grid column past a 390px viewport — that is why chips were sliced, the nav truncated at "MILI" (its scroll fade/chevron rendered offscreen), and the popup drifted right (its `left:50%` resolved against the ~460px box). Fix: `min-width:0` on `#hud-top`, `#hub`, `#nav`, and `#hud-top > *`. Nav also gains `scroll-snap-type: x proximity` + per-button snap-align.
- **Menu popup recentering:** `left:0; right:0; margin-inline:auto; width:min(460px, calc(100vw − 16px))` (12px margins <720px) — inset centering immune to wide-ancestor drift; popup-up keyframes now translateY-only. Close "×" and Open buttons no longer clip.
- **Panel scroll affordance:** `.menu-popup-body` gets `overflow-y:auto` + iOS momentum scrolling; `.menu-popup::after` draws a 26px bottom fade + "more below" shadow (token-driven, dark-safe); body bottom padding 1.9rem and `.side-panel > :last-child` margin so the last card clears the fade.
- **Allies:** bare em-dash Trade History empty state → `.empty-card` papyrus card ("No trades yet — post an offer on the Wall."); consecutive identical notifications collapse into one card with a gold `×N` chip (`.note-count`), newest timestamp kept.
- **Military:** unit glyphs redrawn — recurve bow + arrow (was pickaxe-ish), spear-behind-round-shield `spearshield` (was key-like), chariot wheel with hub + rim-to-hub spokes. Envoy button renders a true locked state below GH7: `disabled` + `aria-disabled`, dashed `.btn-locked` chrome, inline lock glyph, "yours: LN" copy; the dispatch popup only wires when unlocked.
- **Map:** header no longer leaks the archetype slug — "Your shore: **Delta Mouth Shore** — **Red Ochre** province specialty" (title-cased display names only). New 5-chip legend under the board (City / Monument / Open shore / Your shore / Province border) using miniature `legendDot()` reproductions of the actual marker fills+glyphs, plus a dashed swatch labeling the dashed strokes as province borders. Site markers enlarged ~1.5× (dot r 7.5→11, glyph 9.5→14) with a pairwise collision-relaxation pass (27px min separation, board-edge clamp) fixing the Delta Mouth pile-up.
- **Login:** fields papyrus-tinted `#FBF6E8` with inset shadow, gold ring on `:focus-visible`, serif (display-face) labels; dark-mode variants included. Backdrop sun raised (cy 300→~145) and glow softened/enlarged so it reads as sky above the card instead of a clipped disc behind it.
- **Copy:** Tablets gift field "Amount of unique luxury" → "How much of your unique luxury to send".
- All new components use surface/ink tokens with `html.dark` overrides and keep `:focus-visible` outlines (locked buttons excepted — they are non-interactive by design).
